Stablecoin wallet checker

Enter a wallet address. The page shows how much in stablecoins it holds, how the balance has changed, and which protocols its stablecoins are locked in.

A 0x… address is checked on four EVM networks, a Solana address on Solana. Nothing is signed or sent from the wallet.

What you will see after the check
  • The balance of each stablecoin per network and the total in dollars.
  • A chart of the total stablecoin balance over time.
  • Whether an issuer (Circle, Tether, Paxos) has blocked the address, and which contracts are allowed to spend stablecoins from the wallet.
  • How much in stablecoins is sitting idle and what it would earn at current yields.
  • Protocol positions: Aave, Compound, Morpho vaults, Sky, Ethena, Pendle, Curve, Kamino and others, recognised by the tokens the wallet holds.

Limitations

On EVM networks the balance is computed from all token transfers. Aave aTokens grow with interest without transfers, so their balance shown is slightly lower than the real one.

Positions that do not issue a token are not visible: direct loans on Morpho Blue, Kamino Lend, marginfi and Drift, and Uniswap v3 positions (NFTs) on EVM networks.

Approvals are checked only for tracked stablecoins the wallet has had transfers in. Approvals given through Uniswap Permit2 show up as one approval for Permit2, without the individual apps behind it.

Tokens the wallet did not buy or lock itself (fake airdrops) are not shown as positions. For vaults and savings tokens (sUSDe, sUSDS) the number of tokens is shown, not their dollar value, unless the price is known.
