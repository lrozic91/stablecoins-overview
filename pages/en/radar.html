Mint radar

Tracks new stablecoin mints on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Robinhood Chain and Solana and announces every new mint with a pop-up notification. The page checks automatically every 30 minutes (or right away with “Check now”) while open and immediately shows mints from the last 30 minutes.

A mint is a token transfer from the zero address (Transfer event); for USDT on Ethereum it is the Issue event. Covered tokens: Ethereum USDT, USDC, USDS, DAI, USDe, PYUSD, USD1, RLUSD, USDG, EURC · Base USDC, EURC, USDe · Arbitrum USDC, USD₮0, PYUSD, USDe · Robinhood Chain USDG, USDe, U.

Solana: USDC, USDT, PYUSD, USDG, USD1, EURC (mintTo instruction). EURC amounts are shown in euros and count 1:1 toward the threshold. Data: Blockscout for EVM networks, Helius for Solana.
