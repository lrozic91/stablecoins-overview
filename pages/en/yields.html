How to read the ranking

APY, not APR. DefiLlama shows APY, the annual yield with compounding. APR is the same yield without reinvesting. Below 10% the difference is small; at 50% APY the equivalent APR is about 41%.

High yield usually means rewards or risk. When the orange part of the bar is large, the yield comes from protocol tokens, whose price and quantity change. The 30-day average shows whether today’s figure is lasting or a short-lived spike.

Small pools jump around. In a pool with low TVL, a single large deposit or withdrawal sharply changes the yield. That’s why the default filter is 1 M $.

Risks aren’t in the number. Every service carries smart contract risk, and some also carry the risk of a depeg, liquidations or funds locked until maturity. The ranking shows yield, not safety.
